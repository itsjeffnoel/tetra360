Tetra 360

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Tetra 360 is a single-player puzzle game. You do not create an account with us.

What we store on your device

  • Coin balance, Remove Ads, and item inventory
  • Settings, career stats, and saved Ice Breaker / Surge progress

This data stays on your device. We do not run a Tetra 360 login server.

Purchases

Coin packs and Remove Ads are sold through Apple. Apple processes payment. We never see your card details. Restore Purchases returns Remove Ads and the Starter Pack if you already bought them.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple may show your nickname and scores on leaderboards. That data is handled by Apple, not by a Tetra 360 server.

Ads and tracking

The app may show ads. On iOS, Apple’s tracking prompt asks before we use the Identifier for Advertisers. You can decline and still play. Remove Ads turns off banner and interstitial ads.

Children

Tetra 360 is a general audience game. It is not directed at children under 13.
